**How it's measured.** Each MP3 is decoded and split into speech and silence from its loudness (20 ms frames, adaptive threshold). The transcript's word timings are then compared with that:
- **lag**: the shift that best lines up transcript speech with audio speech. + means the text is ahead of the voice.
- **drift**: the lag in the first third vs the last third of the track.
- **silent en/zh**: the share of English words / Chinese characters highlighted while no one is speaking.
- **prec**: the share of highlighted time that is real speech.
